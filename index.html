The builder

Tony Papageorgiou

Tony grew up in the swimming pool industry. His father, grandfather and great-uncle were all involved in it, and as a young kid he would go to work with them on weekends. The passion for constructing and designing pools runs deep in this family, and it was clear from early on that he would go on to build his own.

He founded Aegean Pools in 2004. He still builds every pool from start to finish with a hands-on approach, and takes pride in the total construction process with an attention to detail as if it were in his own backyard.

From your initial consultation with Tony, you can rest assured your pool is finished to the highest of standards.
